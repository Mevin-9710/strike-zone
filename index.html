HOW TO PLAY

Learn by doing with Amara, or read the field guide.

FIELD GUIDE

CONTROLS (PC)

WASD move · SHIFT walk quietly · SPACE jump · C slide (while running) · Left click fire · Right click aim · R reload · 1 / 2 or wheel switch weapon · Q / F abilities · Hold E plant / defuse / pick up · T spray · ESC pause

CONTROLS (PHONE)

Left thumb: move stick. Right side: drag to look. On-screen buttons fire, jump, use abilities and pause. Hold the action button to plant or defuse.

MISSIONS

Every mission has a timer. Watch the objective banner at the top and the marker on your minimap.

  • Defuse – reach each device and hold E until the bar fills.
  • Plant & hold – plant the charge on the marked target, then defend it until it blows.
  • Retrieve – grab the drives and carry them to the extraction point.
  • Intel – optional files hidden on each map. Collect them for XP and story.

ABILITIES

Pick two per mission in the Loadout step. Each has a cooldown shown on its button.

  • Smoke – blocks enemy sight lines. Flash – blinds enemies who look at it. Dash – fast burst of movement.
  • Sensor – reveals nearby enemies. Decoy – a fake target that draws enemy fire. Shield – absorbs incoming damage.
  • Lockdown – pins down enemies inside its zone. Drone – a flying scout that reveals enemies around it.

ENEMIES

  • Guard – standard rifleman. Heavy – armored, slow, big damage. Sniper – deadly at range; watch for the red laser.
  • Commander – uses abilities and rallies others. Drone / Turret – fast or fixed fire; flank them.

TIPS

  • Headshots deal the most damage.
  • Green-cross MEDKITS (also on the minimap) heal 35 HP when you walk over them and respawn after 30 s.
  • Walk (SHIFT) to stay quiet; running gets you heard.
  • Reload behind cover, and use smoke before crossing open streets.
  • Finish missions to earn XP and coins for new operatives, weapons and cosmetics.
