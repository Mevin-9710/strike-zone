# Strike Zone — Expansion Plan (from interview, 2026-11-09)

## Maps
- Hand-placed story hubs (objective set-pieces) + procedurally generated filler districts around them. No closed square-wall arenas: open streets that read as a city.
- Multi-level interiors (floors, stairs, rooftops), bigger districts, destructible props.
- Map features drive the story:
  - **Breach to progress**: destroy walls/doors to reach objectives or open shortcuts.
  - **Vertical objectives**: devices/intel on upper floors and rooftops.
  - **Intel collectibles**: hidden intel unlocks story chapters and gear.

## Campaign
- 12+ missions (current 3 plus 9+ new), new districts per chapter.
- New weapons and operatives unlock **through story progress only**.

## Home screen
- 3D operative lobby: chosen operative idles in a scene; tabs Play / Loadout / Shop / Profile.

## Shop & progression
- Shop sells **charms & sprays** for coins.
- XP levels + battle pass; pass rewards are **coins**.
- CrazyGames rewarded ads: **double XP after a mission**, **one revive per mission**.

## Build order (fastest first)
1. 3D lobby, XP/levels, battle pass (coins), shop (charms & sprays), rewarded ads.
2. Map generator: procgen districts around hand-placed hubs, multi-level buildings, breachables, intel.
3. Campaign: missions 4–12+, story unlocks for weapons/operatives.
