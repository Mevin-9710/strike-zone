# Strike Zone: story bible (season 1)

## Premise
Near-future city, Halvane. A rival agency, **Meridian Covenant**, is planting sabotage devices across the city. The player is **Operative Rhea Voss**, a field agent for the **Civic Shield** counter-sabotage unit. Each mission is a plant-or-defuse operation. The devices are never the whole story.

Tone: serious spy thriller. Short briefings, clipped radio lines, no jokes.

## Season 1 (3-4 missions, expandable)

### Mission 1: "First Signal" (opening, ~3 min)
- Objective: defuse a device planted in a rooftop transit hub. Enemies: Covenant guards (bots).
- Briefing: Voss is new to the unit; handler **Dara Okonkwo** gives controls in the field, one line at a time.
- Loadout choice: pick 1 of 3 abilities (smoke, dash, flash). Rifle is fixed.
- Ending (betrayal twist): Okonkwo's voice calls the wrong extraction point. The device was not Covenant's. It carries a Civic Shield signature.
- Cliffhanger line: *"Someone in our unit sent that device the route. Trust no channel but mine."*

### Mission 2: "Low Tide" (~3 min)
- Objective: plant a counter-charge on the Covenant server at a flooded data vault, then hold the area for 30 seconds until it cracks.
- Loadout: pick 2 abilities from a pool of 6 (sensor, smoke, dash, shield, decoy, flash). Mission rewards a new option.
- Story: Voss finds a Covenant courier who knows Okonkwo's real name.
- Ending: the courier is shot by an unseen sniper before talking. The sniper used Civic Shield frequencies.
- Cliffhanger: *"The courier had a message for you, Operative. It was addressed to your old name."*

### Mission 3: "Dead Air" (~4 min)
- Objective: defuse three devices in the city's power grid before the blackout timer ends. Enemies: Covenant heavies.
- Loadout: pick 2 abilities from a pool of 8 (adds a lockdown and a recon drone).
- Ending (season finale for now): Voss reaches the final device and sees Okonkwo on the feed, standing with a Covenant commander. The device is armed to her own call sign.
- Cliffhanger: *"Next time you hear my voice, it will be the last thing you hear."* Shown with a "Season 2 coming soon" card.

## Abilities and mission-based victory (design, not the story itself)
- Each mission defines the player's victory condition (e.g. defuse all devices, hold the vault, reach extraction).
- Abilities come from a shared pool; a mission offers a subset. Players pick two.
- Names are original; no Valorant agent, ability or map names.

## Suspense rules (for each new episode)
1. Open each ending on a reveal that recontextualizes the last one.
2. Never close the central mystery in a single episode.
3. Keep "Season N coming soon" on the final card, with a hook line.

## Research scoring (2026-11-07)
Sources: [ResearchGate/SSRN narrative-immersion studies](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5482183); [serial vs episodic exergame study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12192482); [cliffhanger reader experiments, SUNY](https://researchconnect.suny.edu/en/publications/investigating-responses-to-narrative-cliffhangers-using-affective/); [cross-cultural mobile game study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1875952125000746); [Rigby & Przybylski, Motivational Pull of Video Games](https://www.academia.edu/24486969/The_Motivational_Pull_of_Video_Games_A_Self_Determination_Theory_Approach).

Key findings used:
- Serialized narratives keep players returning more than episodic ones (children's exergame study).
- Cliffhangers increase the desire for the next installment without hurting enjoyment (text experiments; not games).
- More story engagement does not reliably mean higher retention; heavy story consumers were not the best retainers in the mobile study. (Counterpoint; one study.)
- Autonomy and competence predict enjoyment and future play; relatedness matters less in single player.

| **Overall** | **14/25** | Solid hook, weak player agency. |

## Improvements applied
1. **Autonomy:** the loadout choice now sets the route to the mission's final device. A "stealth" pick gives a rooftop route with no alerts, and an "assault" pick leads to a harder but faster approach. The next briefing references the choice.
2. **Competence:** each defuse is tied to a skill: the first mission teaches the hold-to-defuse action and the mission 2 vault teaches timing. A short "+ Intel" line confirms progress.
3. **Relatedness:** Okonkwo speaks through a radio in-mission. Her voice line changes when the player picks the assault route (more pressure).
4. **Length guard:** no cutscene longer than 15 seconds; every briefing is skippable with one key. Story text is shown in the HUD, not as a movie.
5. **Clue in gameplay:** the Civic Shield signature appears on the device's HUD in mission 1, so the player sees the betrayal clue before the ending.

New score (estimate): 19/25. Biggest remaining risk: 3-4 short missions may run out fast; the expansion plan (season 2) is the answer.
