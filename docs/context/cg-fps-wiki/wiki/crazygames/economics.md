# Economics

- No earnings in Basic Launch (ads off). Rev share starts at Full Launch. [S: S2, S7]
- Revenue share percentage is **not public**; reports range 40-60%. [C/B: S9]
- Ads: at most one midgame ad and one rewarded ad per 3 minutes. [B: S9 citing CG ad rules]
- Payouts monthly via Tipalti once above €100. [B: S9 citing CG docs; not verified directly]
- Exclusivity bonus: +50% compensation for two months if the game is on no other browser portal (Developer Terms 5.5). [B: S9; PDF not read]
- Reported yield: ~€1.20 per 1,000 plays from older simple games (2018). Planning range $1-15 per 1,000 sessions. [B: S9, assumption-based]
- Scale claims: 50M+ monthly players (docs) [S: S7]; 320M sessions and 60M MAU per a Korean forum report [B: S9].
- Developer terms say a game must integrate the SDK, have no outside ads, be original to be eligible for rev share. [S: S7]
- Technical SDK support from CrazyGames only after 50k combined plays. [S: S1]

## Implication [I]
Money is not the near-term goal; the Basic Launch metrics are. A 3-5 week dev cost on a heavy 3D game with servers has poor expected value unless Basic Launch odds are good.
