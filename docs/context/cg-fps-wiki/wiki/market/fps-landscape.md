- Krunker.io and Venge.io (character abilities + FPS) described as trending in a 2025 roundup. Venge is the closest existing "ability shooter".
- Veck.io launched December 2025 and already ranks high: new shooters can still break in. [C]
- Audience: teenagers prefer competitive .io games; strong US/UK/AU concentration. [S: S7]

## Reading [I]
- Demand for browser FPS is proven and large, and CrazyGames features multiplayer.
- The top slots are held by games with big established communities and fast-loading, instant-match, small-map formats (Shell Shockers, Krunker, Hazmob). Their session shape is "jump in, die, respawn in seconds".
- A Valorant-like round-based 5v5 is a different shape: longer matches, round elimination (dead players wait), needs 10 players. Slow-to-start matchmaking contradicts the "land in gameplay in seconds" guideline.
- A tactical mode could still differentiate: it may fill the gap between .io deathmatch and heavy PC shooters. But the gap may exist because it is hard to staff with players. [I]
